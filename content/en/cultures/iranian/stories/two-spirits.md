In Yasna 30 two primal spirits appear, twin in origin, one choosing life and truth, the other choosing the lie and destruction. Every person must choose between them.

## Why it matters
It frames existence as an ethical contest rather than a fixed fate. Later Zoroastrian tradition names the destructive spirit Angra Mainyu and develops a full story of cosmic conflict ending in renewal, but that expanded narrative is from later texts.
