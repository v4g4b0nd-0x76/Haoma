<p class="lead">Zoroastrianism is the prophetic religion associated with Zarathustra: a cosmos ordered by <em>asha</em> (truth, right-working) against <em>druj</em> (the Lie), with fire and careful speech as lasting signs of the good.</p>

Pages tagged to this tradition gather characters, stories and history that sit inside that frame — without pretending every Iranian myth is Zoroastrian.
