<p class="lead">Mehr (Avestan <em>Miθra</em>) is the yazata of contract and daylight in the Iranian sources — a thousand ears and ten thousand eyes, and no oath-breaker escapes him.</p>

This tradition page gathers material on Iranian Mihr worship and covenant theology. For the Roman mystery cult that took the name Mithras, see <a href="/cultures/iranian/traditions/mithraism/">Mithraism</a>.
