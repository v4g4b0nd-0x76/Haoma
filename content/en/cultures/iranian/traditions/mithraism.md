<p class="lead">Mithraism here means the Roman mystery cult of Mithras: tauroctony reliefs, grades of initiation, and a Latin-speaking empire — not a simple copy of Avestan Mehr.</p>

Scholars disagree on how much Iranian religion fed the Roman cult. Haoma keeps <strong>Mehr</strong> and <strong>Mithraism</strong> as sister traditions so the name link stays visible without collapsing the two.
