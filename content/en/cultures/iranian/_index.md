Ancient Iranian religion is known mainly through the Avesta, the sacred texts of Zoroastrianism. Its world is built on a struggle between order and truth (*asha*) and falsehood (*druj*), with figures such as the prophet Zarathustra and the divine guardian of oaths, Mehr.
