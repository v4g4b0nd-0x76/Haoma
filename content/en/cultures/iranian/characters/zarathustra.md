<p class="lead">Zarathustra, known in Greek as Zoroaster, is the prophet to whom the Gathas, the oldest Avestan hymns, are attributed.</p>

## Who was he?
Almost everything about his life is uncertain. The language of the Gathas points to a very early date, and linguists often suggest a range between about 1500 and 1000 BCE. Zoroastrian tradition instead places him in the 6th century BCE. His homeland was probably in eastern Iran or Central Asia.

## Teaching
The Gathas present Ahura Mazda, the "Wise Lord", as the supreme god. Humans choose between *asha* (truth, order) and *druj* (the lie), and that choice shapes the fate of the world. See [The Two Spirits](/cultures/iranian/stories/two-spirits/).

## Tradition
Later texts add legends of his miraculous birth, his visions and his patron King Vishtaspa. These are traditional accounts and not evidence from the Gathas themselves.
