<p class="lead">Mehr, Avestan <em>Miθra</em>, is the yazata (divine being worthy of worship) who guards covenants, truth and the light of day.</p>

## Name and meaning
The Avestan word *miθra* means "contract" or "agreement". The god is the personification of that bond, so breaking an oath offends him directly.

## In the Mihr Yasht
The hymn to Mehr describes a watchful, ever-awake lord with a thousand ears and ten thousand eyes. He rides ahead of the sun, sees every liar, and punishes those who break their word. He rewards honest dealing and protects the righteous.

> A world without trust cannot stand, so the guardian of trust never sleeps.

## Festival
The seventh month of the Iranian calendar is named Mehr, and its sixteenth day is celebrated as **Mehregan**, an autumn festival of harvest, gratitude and friendship that is still observed today.

## Relation to Roman Mithras
The Roman cult of Mithras shares the name, but scholars disagree about how much of Mehr's character carried over. Treat the two as related by name, not as one religion.
