Zoroastrian ideas spread across the Iranian world and shaped the Achaemenid empire. Under the Sasanian dynasty (224 to 651 CE) it became a state religion, and the Avesta was written down in its own script. After the Arab conquest the community shrank but survived, in Iran and in India as the Parsis.
