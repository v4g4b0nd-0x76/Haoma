The myths of Sumer, Akkad, Babylon and Assyria survive on clay tablets written in cuneiform. The best known are the Epic of Gilgamesh, the Enuma Elish (the Babylonian creation story, in which Marduk defeats Tiamat) and the Descent of Inanna.
