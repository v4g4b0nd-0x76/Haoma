<p class="lead">When Amaterasu seals herself in the Heavenly Rock Cave, daylight fails. The other kami do not storm the rock — they feast, dance and laugh until curiosity opens the door to light again.</p>

## Ritual as plot

Uzume’s dance, the uproar of the kami, and the mirror held before the cave mouth are not comic filler. They are a myth of how community and rite restore what violence hid. Later shrine practice remembers the mirror as more than furniture.

{{< recon >}}
Modern retellings that treat the cave only as a “solar eclipse allegory” flatten the chronicle’s ritual logic. Keep the dance and the mirror first; allegory second.
{{< /recon >}}
