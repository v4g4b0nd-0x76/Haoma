<p class="lead">In Izumo, Susanoo faces Yamata no Orochi — an eight-headed, eight-tailed serpent that demands a daughter each year. Trickery and sake clear the way for the sword that ends the hunger.</p>

## The fight

Susanoo builds a fence with eight gates, sets vats of sake, and waits. As the heads drink, he cuts. In the tail he finds a great sword, which he presents upward — the blade later called Kusanagi in imperial memory.
