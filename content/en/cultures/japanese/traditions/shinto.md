<p class="lead">Shinto is the living field of kami, purification and shrine festival — continuous with the chronicles’ world, but not identical to any single ancient book.</p>

## Continuity and caution

Local shrines, household rites and imperial ceremonial share vocabulary (*kami*, *harae*, *matsuri*) across centuries. Still, “Shinto” as a neat system is partly a modern word-work. Haoma separates mythic charter texts from Meiji-era State Shinto politics.
