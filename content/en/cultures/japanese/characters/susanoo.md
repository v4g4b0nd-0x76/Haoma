<p class="lead">Susanoo is the unruly storm kami — brother to the sun — whose exile from heaven leads to the Izumo serpent fight and the sword later named among the imperial treasures.</p>

## Offence and exile

After wrecking the rice fields and weaving hall of heaven, Susanoo is driven out. The chronicles treat him as both destroyer and culture hero: the same figure who darkens the sun later clears the land of a devouring serpent.

## Izumo

In Izumo he meets a family whose daughters are being fed to Yamata no Orochi. He transforms one into a comb, intoxicates the eight-headed serpent with sake, and cuts it apart — discovering a sword in the tail. That blade becomes *Kusanagi*, folded into later imperial legend.
