<p class="lead">Amaterasu is the sun kami of the Plain of High Heaven. In the court chronicles she is both light itself and the genealogical root claimed by early Yamato rulers.</p>

## Sun and sovereignty

The *Kojiki* and *Nihon Shoki* place Amaterasu at the centre of a divine family whose descendants become earthly sovereigns. That claim is political theology as much as myth — say which chronicle you follow when details diverge.

{{< disagree >}}
- **Kojiki:** often sharper narrative of the cave, the mirror, and Susanoo’s offences.
- **Nihon Shoki:** multiple variant tellings side by side — useful when sources disagree.
{{< /disagree >}}

## The rock cave

When Susanoo’s violence darkens heaven, Amaterasu seals herself in a cave. The other kami dance, laugh and hold up a mirror until curiosity draws her light back into the world. The episode is ritual memory: mirror, jewel and sword later appear among the imperial regalia.
