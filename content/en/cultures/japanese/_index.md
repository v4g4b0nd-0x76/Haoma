Japanese myth is known mainly through the *Kojiki* (712) and *Nihon Shoki* (720), court chronicles that braid kami genealogies with early rulers. The world is thick with *kami* — not only “gods,” but spirits of place, craft and lineage — and with islands born from divine coupling and purification.
