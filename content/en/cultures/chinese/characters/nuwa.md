<p class="lead">Nüwa is the craftsman of beginnings — shaping people from clay and, when the sky breaks, melting five-coloured stones to seal the wound of heaven.</p>

## Maker and mender

Chinese sources do not give one canonical “Book of Nüwa.” Layers gather in the *Huainanzi* and later mythographies: creation, marriage with Fuxi in some lines, and the famous repair of the sky after Gonggong smashes a pillar of heaven.
