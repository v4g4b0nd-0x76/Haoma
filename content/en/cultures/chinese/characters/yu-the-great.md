<p class="lead">Yu the Great is the flood hero who does not dam the waters forever — he leads them through channels until the plain can be lived on again.</p>

## Labour as virtue

Tradition says Yu passed his own door three times without entering while the work lasted. Whether Xia is archaeology or charter myth, Yu remains the Chinese figure of public labour against chaos.

{{< recon >}}
Equating Yu’s channels one-for-one with modern hydrology maps is a reconstruction habit. Keep the moral of ordered waters first; geographic overlays second and labelled.
