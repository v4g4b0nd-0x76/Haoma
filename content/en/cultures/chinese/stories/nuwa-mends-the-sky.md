<p class="lead">When heaven’s pillar breaks, the world lists toward flood and flame. Nüwa answers not with another war but with craft — fire, colour and stone fused into a patch for the sky.</p>

## Craft over conquest

The episode is a theology of repair. Where other myths crown a storm-killer, this one crowns a maker. The five colours are not decoration; they are the spectrum of a mended order.
