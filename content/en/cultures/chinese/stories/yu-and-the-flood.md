<p class="lead">The flood will not obey a wall alone. Yu learns to walk with the water — cutting channels, naming rivers, returning the plain to grain and towns.</p>

## From chaos to mandate

In later political reading, Yu’s success proves fitness to rule. Haoma keeps the labour story readable on its own, then points to the Mandate page for the Zhou theology that generalised Heaven’s judgment on rulers.
