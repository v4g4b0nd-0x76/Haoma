<p class="lead">The Mandate of Heaven is the Zhou argument that rule is conditional — virtue keeps the charge; cruelty invites its loss.</p>

## A political sky

Heaven here is moral weather, not only astronomy. Dynasties after Zhou inherited the vocabulary. Haoma treats the early charter seriously and flags later imperial uses as historical layers.
