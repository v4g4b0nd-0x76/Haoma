<p class="lead">Rama is the prince of Ayodhya whose exile, the abduction of Sita, and the war for Lanka form the spine of the <em>Ramayana</em>.</p>

## Which Ramayana?

Indian epic tradition is plural. Valmiki’s Sanskrit poem is a classical reference; Tamil, Hindi and Southeast Asian tellings reshape scenes and morals. Haoma pages should name the line they follow.

{{< disagree >}}
- **Valmiki tradition:** Rama as ideal king bound by dharma — including painful choices about Sita’s ordeal.
- **Devotional retellings:** often emphasise Rama as avatar of Vishnu and soften or reframesome court episodes.
{{< /disagree >}}

## Exile and vow

Denied the throne by a palace promise, Rama accepts forest exile with Sita and Lakshmana. The forest is not escape: it is where dharma is tested by demons, sages and loss.
