<p class="lead">When Sita is held in Lanka, Rama’s allies raise a bridge across the sea so the army can cross. The episode is engineering as devotion — stones that float because names and vows are laid into them.</p>

## The crossing

Nala and the vanaras cast mountains into the ocean. The poem treats the bridge as both marvel and military road. Later storytellers point to the chain of shoals between India and Sri Lanka; that identification is a living tradition, not a surveyor’s proof inside the Sanskrit text.
