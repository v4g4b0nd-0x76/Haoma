<p class="lead">Athena is the Olympian of wisdom, weaving and skilled war — the defence of the city rather than battlefield frenzy.</p>

## Birth

In Hesiod, Zeus swallows the goddess Metis; Athena springs fully armed from his head. The story marks her as mind-born: strategy and craft inside the king’s household of gods.

## City and craft

Athens claims her as patron. Olive, loom and the bronze of orderly war sit beside the owl. She helps Odysseus and others with counsel — a pattern distinct from Ares’ rage.
