<p class="lead">Zeus is king of the Olympian gods and ruler of the sky. Hesiod’s <em>Theogony</em> is the main early genealogical poem; Homer’s epics show him already enthroned among the living myths of war and hospitality.</p>

## Birth and kingship

Kronos swallows his children; Rhea hides Zeus in Crete. Grown, Zeus forces the swallowed gods free and leads the war against the Titans. Victory makes him distributor of honours among the gods — not a solitary creator, but the head of a household of powers.

## Oath and guest-right

Besides the thunderbolt, Zeus watches *xenia* (guest-friendship) and broken oaths. That ethical role is as central in the poems as the weather.
