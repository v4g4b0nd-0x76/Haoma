Haoma is a hobby podcast and open wiki about the myths of Iran and the wider East, built on primary sources.

The name is the Avestan word for the sacred plant pressed in the Yasna ritual, and for the divinity praised in the Hom Yasht (Yasna 9 to 11). Its Vedic cousin is Soma. We borrow it for what the drink stood for in the texts: renewal, healing and the memory of stories.

Everything is open source. Corrections are welcome through GitHub issues and pull requests. Every page lists its sources and is graded: primary text, later compilation, archaeology or modern scholarship.
