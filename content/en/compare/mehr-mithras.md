## Shared ground

Both names go back to an Indo-Iranian root for *contract* / *agreement*. Iranian Mehr (Avestan *Miθra*) is a yazata of oaths and daylight in the Avesta. Roman Mithras appears in a later mystery cult across the empire, with its own rituals and images.

## Where they part

{{< disagree >}}
- **Iranian Mehr:** chariot ahead of the sun, guardian of covenants; no bull-slaying scene in the Avesta.
- **Roman Mithras:** famous for the *tauroctony* (bull-slaying) in cave-shrines; the link to Iranian Mehr is debated — shared origin versus Hellenistic reinvention.
