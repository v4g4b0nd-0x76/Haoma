## Shared ground

Both stand at the head of a divine household and are linked to the sky, sovereignty and hard bargains with fate or law. Comparative myth sometimes groups them as “chief gods,” which is a teaching shorthand.

## Where they part

{{< disagree >}}
- **Odin:** seeks wisdom through self-harm and wandering; death at Ragnarok is part of the song.
- **Zeus:** consolidates rule after Titanomachy; Homeric Zeus is not marching toward a fated death of the gods.
{{< /disagree >}}
